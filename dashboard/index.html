Implied value = TVL × fitted multiple. The range is the calibrated 80% interval from leave-one-out residuals — wide on purpose: the median live multiple is typically missed by a factor of three, and this board says so instead of hiding it.

These protocols already have a token that trades. They are not on the board — they are the comparables the model is fitted from. Separating them from the pre-token board is the point: a launch radar ranks what has not launched yet.

How the number is made

For each live token, multiple = market cap ÷ TVL. radar-4 fits log(multiple) by ridge regression on log TVL, seven-day TVL change, log age and category, with a separate shrinkage penalty per group. Leave-one-out refits the model 119 times, each time holding out one live token and predicting it. Walk-forward trains only on tokens listed before a cutoff date and tests on tokens listed after it — the closest thing to predicting the future this snapshot allows. The baseline is a TVL-distance weighted median multiple. radar-4 beats it on both, and the gap is widest walk-forward, where the old growth and maturity multipliers hurt most.

What this board does not claim

  • It does not predict whether or when a protocol issues a token. “Pre-token” means the snapshot records no token, never that the protocol has none.
  • The median miss is large: half of all live multiples are missed by a factor of about three. Use the range and the verdict, not the point number alone.
  • Wrapped, staked, bridged and versioned products are left out on purpose, as are exchanges — they are not launch candidates.
